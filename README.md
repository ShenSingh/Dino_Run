# 🦖 Dino_Run

Dino_Run is a simple and fun side-scrolling game made in **Godot Engine**, inspired by the classic Flappy Bird concept. Tap to jump, avoid flying birds and ground obstacles, and try to beat your high score!

## 🎮 Gameplay

- Control a pixel-art dinosaur.
- Press **Spacebar** or tap the **up button** to jump.
- Avoid flying birds and ground obstacles (like rocks and tree stumps).
- Game ends if you touch any obstacle or bird.
- Background features a looping forest scene.
- The game includes:
  - Jump sound
  - Background music (optional)
  - Score and High Score tracking
  - Restart screen on game over
